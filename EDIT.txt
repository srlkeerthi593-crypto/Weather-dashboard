BACKGROUND ONLY - 2 steps

1) Upload background.py into your  utils/  folder (next to style.py and data_loader.py).

2) Easiest (applies to ALL pages at once): open utils/style.py, find the function  inject_css()
   and add these two lines at the very END of that function (indented like the rest of the function):

       from utils.background import apply_background
       apply_background()

   If you prefer not to touch style.py, add this instead in app.py (and in each page file),
   right after the line  inject_css():

       from utils.background import apply_background
       apply_background()

Reboot the app. Nothing else changes.
